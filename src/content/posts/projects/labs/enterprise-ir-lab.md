The aim of this lab is not to build another SIEM or EDR. I want to practice the investigation workflow: collecting evidence, scoping incidents, containment, recovery, and reporting.

With limited hardware, every virtual machine needs a purpose. I’m keeping the setup small and the scenarios repeatable.

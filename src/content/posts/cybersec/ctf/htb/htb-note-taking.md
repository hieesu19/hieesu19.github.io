I wanted a consistent way to document labs without losing commands or screenshots in a long, unstructured file.

When I notice something new, I write down what I tried, what happened, and what I want to test next. That makes the final writeup much easier to put together.

```text
notes/
├── recon.md
├── enumeration.md
├── screenshots/
└── findings.md
```

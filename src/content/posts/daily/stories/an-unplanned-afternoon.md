I had been at my laptop since morning. By late afternoon, the light had changed and I realized I had barely moved. I closed everything and went outside.

> Not every day needs a highlight.

I took the familiar streets slowly. A bicycle leaned against an old wall. Someone was watering plants by the doorway. People were talking over iced tea. Nothing especially memorable happened, which was exactly what I needed.

I came home with no photos worth posting and nothing crossed off a list. Still, it was a good afternoon.
